02 · About

Why Choose Hari University?

India's first greenfield, privately sponsored Deemed-to-be University, approved by the Government of India under UGC's Distinct Category.

Interdisciplinary by design

Students work alongside peers from Agriculture, Engineering, Management and Philosophy from Day 1.

Research from semester one

Every student steps into an ongoing investigation from their very first semester — not preparing to do research, already doing it.

A living laboratory

A several-hundred-acre regenerated campus at Kanha Shanti Vanam, wired with IoT sensors — a dataset no classroom can replicate.

Heartfulness Curriculum

Brighter Minds, HELP, HELM and HOPE — frameworks for cognition, life skills, leadership and personal excellence.

Ethics at the core

Ethics is a credit-bearing core subject in every B.Tech — not an elective, not a footnote.

Fully residential

Every programme is fully residential — learning, practice and community life share one campus.

Leadership

Guided by purpose

The leadership shaping Hari University's vision of education, research and inner growth.

Vice Chancellor

Dr. Narsireddy Malgireddy

Leading Hari University's academic vision — interdisciplinary learning, research from the first semester, and education rooted in Heartfulness values.
