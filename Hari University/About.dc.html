Where consciousness meets research.

Hari University — the Heartfulness Academy of Research & Innovation — is India's first greenfield, privately sponsored Deemed-to-be University, approved by the Government of India under UGC's Distinct Category.

Our philosophy

Skills for a career matter. Skills for a life matter more.

Most universities teach theory first and hope it reaches the world eventually. At Hari University, we start with what the world needs and work backwards.

Our curriculum builds on 75 years of Heartfulness practice — in farms, schools, hospitals and boardrooms across 160 countries — and every degree is earned on a living campus at Kanha Shanti Vanam, Telangana.
